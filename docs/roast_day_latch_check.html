Energy latch check — one 600 g

Branch refine_hybrid_control @ 7f207eb3d. Backend stays Energy. Do not change PID, slews, schedule, or fan settings. Background is eyes only.

Setup

  • Hybrid Controller on. Backend Energy. ET/BT = Kaleido.
  • One 600 g charge. Last week’s .alog on the Artisan background.
  • Warmup SV 195. Start Heating. Hold 25+ min after SV is first reached.
  • START, then CHARGE as you dump. Mark DRY, FCs, and DROP. Do not touch the sliders.

The latch does not use charge BT. After CHARGE it stays in Charge until BT has been below 100 °C, or 90 s, or you mark DRY. Last roast labeled a hot charge as Maillard, so heater and fan slammed in the first half minute and FCs RoR sat near 14 °C/min instead of about 10.

Watch

WhenGoodBad — note it, or abort
First ~30 s after CHARGE Heater high and steady. Fan low. No flicker. Heater 48→5→95 or fan 30→0→70
Drying Fan sits around 20–30%. Heater high. RoR climbs, then eases. Fan chattering
FCs window RoR already declining, about 8–11 °C/min. Fan steps up after crack. RoR still ~14, or a crash
DROP Heater snaps to 0 within a few seconds. Heat lingers

Phase is not on screen. Judge the latch from that first-30-seconds actuator trace. If the heater runs away, the fan stalls, or it oscillates fast: abort to Machine PID. Do not retune mid-roast.
